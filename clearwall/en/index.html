How it works

Transparent Wallpaper

Shows the rear camera view as your wallpaper, as if your phone were see-through.

Selfie Mode

Switch to the front camera and check your look right from your lock or home screen.

Sticker Studio

Add gallery images as stickers and place as many as you like.

Easy Editing

Pinch to resize and rotate at once. Use the preview to avoid your app icons.

One-Tap Apply

Set it as your live wallpaper with a single tap.
